# PRD Prompt

Act as a senior product manager and UX strategist. Create a complete Product Requirements Document (PRD) for “Amador Auto Care,” a responsive website for an auto repair and body-work business.

Technical constraints:

- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only
- Hosted on GitHub Pages
- No backend server
- Prefer free or low-cost third-party services for forms, appointments, maps, and analytics
- Accessible, mobile-first, fast, SEO-friendly, and easy to maintain
- Do not assume unsupported business details; clearly label open questions and placeholders

Business goals:

- Generate qualified leads
- Make contacting the shop quick and simple
- Let customers request estimates and appointments
- Clearly communicate services, trust, location, hours, and contact information
- Support both mechanical repair and collision/body-work customers

Include these PRD sections:

1. Executive summary
2. Problem statement
3. Business goals and measurable success metrics
4. Target customers and personas
5. Primary customer journeys
6. Scope: MVP, future releases, and explicitly out-of-scope items
7. Information architecture and navigation
8. Page-by-page requirements
9. Functional requirements with IDs, priorities, and acceptance criteria
10. Non-functional requirements
11. Content requirements and content still needed from the business
12. UX/UI direction
13. Accessibility requirements aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA
14. Local SEO and structured-data requirements
15. Performance targets using Core Web Vitals
16. Privacy, security, spam prevention, and form-data handling
17. GitHub Pages deployment and technical architecture
18. Analytics and conversion tracking plan
19. Testing and browser/device compatibility
20. Risks, dependencies, assumptions, and mitigations
21. Launch checklist
22. Post-launch roadmap
23. Open questions for the business owner

At minimum, evaluate requirements for:

- Home page
- Mechanical services
- Body and collision repair services
- About and trust indicators
- Before-and-after gallery
- Reviews/testimonials
- Contact page with click-to-call and directions
- Estimate request form
- Appointment request form
- Business hours and emergency/after-hours guidance
- FAQ
- Privacy policy
- Confirmation, validation, error, loading, and empty states
- English/Spanish support as an option

For estimate requests, consider vehicle details, service category, damage description, preferred contact method, and optional photo uploads. Explain any limitations photo uploads create for a static GitHub Pages site and recommend suitable solutions.

For appointment requests, clarify that submissions are requests rather than guaranteed bookings unless a third-party scheduling platform provides real-time availability.

Prioritize requirements using Must/Should/Could/Won’t. Write testable acceptance criteria in Given/When/Then format. Add a requirements traceability table connecting business goals, user needs, features, metrics, and acceptance criteria.

Conclude with:

- A concise recommended MVP
- Key product decisions requiring owner approval
- 15 prioritized discovery questions
- A phased implementation plan suitable for a small vanilla web project

Write the PRD in clear Markdown. Make concrete recommendations and explain important tradeoffs briefly.
