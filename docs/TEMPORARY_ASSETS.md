## Production Release Blocker

Before a production release, replace every temporary illustration with owner-approved Amador Auto Care imagery. For each replacement, record the owner or license source, usage permission, descriptive caption, dimensions, alternative text, and confirmation that it does not expose customer names, faces, VINs, or license plates without documented consent.

Do not use stock imagery in Gallery, Reviews, testimonials, before-and-after work, or any area that could imply it is proof of Amador Auto Care workmanship.
