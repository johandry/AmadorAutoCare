Privacy

Privacy

We use Supabase to process estimate and appointment requests. No production analytics integration has been selected.

Service requests

When you submit a request, we collect the contact, vehicle, and service details you provide so we can respond. We review new requests at least every four hours. Completed service requests are deleted after 90 days. Active requests are kept until the request is completed.

Your choices and questions

To ask about a service request or privacy, email service@info.amadorautocare.com. Do not include sensitive payment information in a service request. Photo uploads and production analytics are not enabled.
