Privacy

Privacy

This notice explains how online estimate and appointment requests are handled.

Information we collect

When you submit a service request, we collect the contact, vehicle, service, scheduling, and notes details you provide. We use this information only to review and respond to your request.

Processor and storage

Supabase processes and stores service requests. The website sends requests to a secured Supabase Edge Function; website visitors cannot directly read request records.

Retention

We review new requests at least every four hours. Active requests are kept until the request is completed. Completed service requests are deleted after 90 days.

Your choices and questions

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of a service request, email service@info.amadorautocare.com. Do not include sensitive payment information in a service request.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is selected for aggregate page analytics. It remains disabled until its public beacon token is configured. We do not send service-request details or form values to analytics.

Features not enabled

Photo uploads are not enabled. We do not sell service-request information.

Development demo notice

Some business information on this development site is sample content. Before production release, the owner must replace sample values and review this notice with qualified counsel where legal assurance is needed.
